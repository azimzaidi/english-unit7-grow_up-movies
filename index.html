Four Movies. Hidden Clues. One Junior Detective.

Welcome, detective! You will learn to form questions, investigate facts about four movies, and solve four movie mysteries. (Anda akan belajar membina soalan dan menyelesaikan empat misteri filem.)

Work independently. Write your answers in your English exercise book. (Buat sendiri. Tulis jawapan dalam buku latihan Bahasa Inggeris.)

Mission 1: Train Your Detective Brain

A question starts with a question word. Look at the clues:

Three patterns

1. What / Where / When / Who / How + did + subject + base verb?
Where did Moana live?

2. Was / Were + subject + complement?
Was Dory a blue fish? Were Anna and Elsa sisters?

3. Did + subject + base verb?
Did Moana sail across the ocean?

Remember: after DID, the verb goes back to its base form (live, not lived). Questions with WAS and WERE do not use DID.
(Selepas DID, guna kata kerja asas. WAS/WERE tidak guna DID.)

Common mistakes

Where did Moana lived? → Where did Moana live?

What Elsa was? → What was Elsa like? This asks about her characteristics (for example, kind or brave), not what she was doing.

Practice: choose the correct question
